## 1. The question

dApps need a **bill that does not reopen a wallet** and does not send the user to Circle.

That is not the same question as:

- “can I mint a dollar on Kaspa?”

If you mix those questions you get a pitch. Darwin keeps them apart.

## 3. What “beyond the blockchain” means here

The chain is the court. The product is the scene:

- A household jar that does not include an FX cell.
- A plant / control room that bills the event.
- An agent that pays HTTP 402 without opening Kasware.
- A counter that is the same URL as the email invoice.
- A phone that finishes the pay. PC is the teaching bench.

If that scene needs Circle, the PoC failed. If that scene needs a new wallet tab per job, the PoC failed.
